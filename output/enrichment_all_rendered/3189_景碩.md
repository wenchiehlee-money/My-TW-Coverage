# 3189 - 景碩

## 業務簡介
**板塊:** Technology
**產業:** Semiconductor Equipment & Materials
**市值:** 303,503 百萬台幣
**企業價值:** 303,552 百萬台幣

景碩科技 (3189) 是全球 [[Flip Chip]] ([[覆晶封裝]]) IC 載板前三大供應商，為 [![和碩](https://img.shields.io/badge/%E5%92%8C%E7%A2%A9-blue)](4938_%E5%92%8C%E7%A2%A9.md) 轉投資公司。公司主力產品為 [[BGA]] 基板、[![ABF 載板](https://img.shields.io/badge/ABF%20%E8%BC%89%E6%9D%BF-green)](../themes/ABF_%E8%BC%89%E6%9D%BF.md) 及 [[BT 載板]]，服務全球頂級 IC 設計及 IDM 客戶。子公司[[晶碩]] (6491) 專攻高階 [![ABF 載板](https://img.shields.io/badge/ABF%20%E8%BC%89%E6%9D%BF-green)](../themes/ABF_%E8%BC%89%E6%9D%BF.md)。生產據點涵蓋台灣新屋、新豐及蘇州。

## 供應鏈位置
**上游 (基板材料):**
- **ABF 材料:** [[Ajinomoto]] (Ajinomoto，ABF 膜供應)
- **BT 材料:** BT 樹脂基板材料
- **[[銅箔]]/化學品:** PCB 製程原料

**中游 (載板製造):**
- **IC 載板:** **景碩** (Flip Chip BGA/ABF/[[BT 載板]])
- **子公司:** [[晶碩]] (高階 [![ABF 載板](https://img.shields.io/badge/ABF%20%E8%BC%89%E6%9D%BF-green)](../themes/ABF_%E8%BC%89%E6%9D%BF.md))

**下游 (IC 設計與封裝):**
- **IC 設計:** [![Qualcomm](https://img.shields.io/badge/Qualcomm-blue)](QCOM_Qualcomm.md)、[![AMD](https://img.shields.io/badge/AMD-blue)](AMD_AMD.md)、[![NVIDIA](https://img.shields.io/badge/NVIDIA-blue)](NVDA_NVIDIA.md)、[![Broadcom](https://img.shields.io/badge/Broadcom-blue)](AVGO_Broadcom.md)、[[TI]]、[![聯發科](https://img.shields.io/badge/%E8%81%AF%E7%99%BC%E7%A7%91-blue)](2454_%E8%81%AF%E7%99%BC%E7%A7%91.md)、[![Micron](https://img.shields.io/badge/Micron-blue)](MU_Micron.md)
- **終端品牌:** [![Apple](https://img.shields.io/badge/Apple-blue)](AAPL_Apple.md)、[![Samsung](https://img.shields.io/badge/Samsung-blue)](005930.KS_Samsung.md)
- **母公司:** [![和碩](https://img.shields.io/badge/%E5%92%8C%E7%A2%A9-blue)](4938_%E5%92%8C%E7%A2%A9.md) (最大股東)
