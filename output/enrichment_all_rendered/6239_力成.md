# 6239_力成

## 業務簡介
**板塊:** Technology
**產業:** Semiconductors
**市值:** 172,140 百萬台幣
**企業價值:** 204,293 百萬台幣

力成科技股份有限公司 (6239) 是全球前五大、同時也是全球「最大」的 [![DRAM/記憶體](https://img.shields.io/badge/DRAM%2F%E8%A8%98%E6%86%B6%E9%AB%94-green)](../themes/DRAM.md)[[半導體]]封裝測試廠。力成的核心業務涵蓋晶片凸塊、晶圓測試、IC封裝及系統級組裝，提供一站式(Turn-key)的封測服務。面對[[AI]]與高效能運算(HPC)的強勁需求，力成積極佈局先進封裝技術，包含高頻寬 [![DRAM/記憶體](https://img.shields.io/badge/DRAM%2F%E8%A8%98%E6%86%B6%E9%AB%94-green)](../themes/DRAM.md)([![DRAM/記憶體](https://img.shields.io/badge/DRAM%2F%E8%A8%98%E6%86%B6%E9%AB%94-green)](../themes/DRAM.md))封裝、2.5D/3D封裝以及扇出型面板級封裝([[FOPLP]])。公司與美光([![Micron](https://img.shields.io/badge/Micron-blue)](MU_Micron.md))、SK海力士等全球 [![DRAM/記憶體](https://img.shields.io/badge/DRAM%2F%E8%A8%98%E6%86%B6%E9%AB%94-green)](../themes/DRAM.md) 巨頭結為緊密戰略夥伴，並承接 [![台積電](https://img.shields.io/badge/%E5%8F%B0%E7%A9%8D%E9%9B%BB-blue)](2330_%E5%8F%B0%E7%A9%8D%E9%9B%BB.md)[![CoWoS](https://img.shields.io/badge/CoWoS-green)](../themes/CoWoS.md) 封裝外溢訂單，是[[AI]][![DRAM/記憶體](https://img.shields.io/badge/DRAM%2F%E8%A8%98%E6%86%B6%E9%AB%94-green)](../themes/DRAM.md) 供應鏈的關鍵推手。

## 供應鏈位置
*   **上游**: 全球各大 [![DRAM/記憶體](https://img.shields.io/badge/DRAM%2F%E8%A8%98%E6%86%B6%E9%AB%94-green)](../themes/DRAM.md) 製造商(IDM/Fabless)、晶圓代工廠(Foundry)及各類封裝材料/設備供應商。
*   **中游**: **力成科技** ([![DRAM/記憶體](https://img.shields.io/badge/DRAM%2F%E8%A8%98%E6%86%B6%E9%AB%94-green)](../themes/DRAM.md)/[![DRAM/記憶體](https://img.shields.io/badge/DRAM%2F%E8%A8%98%E6%86%B6%E9%AB%94-green)](../themes/DRAM.md)[![DRAM/記憶體](https://img.shields.io/badge/DRAM%2F%E8%A8%98%E6%86%B6%E9%AB%94-green)](../themes/DRAM.md)、邏輯IC之封測代工，及 [![DRAM/記憶體](https://img.shields.io/badge/DRAM%2F%E8%A8%98%E6%86%B6%E9%AB%94-green)](../themes/DRAM.md)/[[FOPLP]]先進封裝技術研發製造)。
*   **下游**: 個人電腦、[[伺服器]]、智慧型手機、車用電子、雲端 [![資料中心](https://img.shields.io/badge/%E8%B3%87%E6%96%99%E4%B8%AD%E5%BF%83-green)](../themes/%E8%B3%87%E6%96%99%E4%B8%AD%E5%BF%83.md) 及各類[[消費性電子]]產品製造商。
