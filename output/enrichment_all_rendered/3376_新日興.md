# 3376 - 新日興

## 業務簡介
**板塊:** Technology
**產業:** Electronic Components
**市值:** 33,087 百萬台幣
**企業價值:** 29,968 百萬台幣

新日興 (3376，Shin Zu Shing / SZS) 為全球精密鉸鏈龍頭，核心客戶 [![Apple](https://img.shields.io/badge/Apple-blue)](AAPL_Apple.md) (MacBook鉸鏈/iPad鍵盤/AirPods充電盒)。與 [![鴻海](https://img.shields.io/badge/%E9%B4%BB%E6%B5%B7-blue)](2317_%E9%B4%BB%E6%B5%B7.md) 合資取得 [![Apple](https://img.shields.io/badge/Apple-blue)](AAPL_Apple.md) 摺疊iPhone (iPhone Fold) 鉸鏈約65%訂單 (另35%由[[Amphenol]]供應)，採液態金屬技術，預計2026下半年推出。另供 [![Dell](https://img.shields.io/badge/Dell-blue)](DELL_Dell.md)、[[Lenovo]]、[![Samsung](https://img.shields.io/badge/Samsung-blue)](005930.KS_Samsung.md)、[![HP Inc.](https://img.shields.io/badge/HP%20Inc.-blue)](HPQ_HP%20Inc..md)、[![Microsoft](https://img.shields.io/badge/Microsoft-blue)](MSFT_Microsoft.md)。組裝廠含 [![廣達](https://img.shields.io/badge/%E5%BB%A3%E9%81%94-blue)](2382_%E5%BB%A3%E9%81%94.md)、[![鴻海](https://img.shields.io/badge/%E9%B4%BB%E6%B5%B7-blue)](2317_%E9%B4%BB%E6%B5%B7.md)、[![仁寶](https://img.shields.io/badge/%E4%BB%81%E5%AF%B6-blue)](2324_%E4%BB%81%E5%AF%B6.md)。另供中國手機品牌水滴型摺疊鉸鏈。核心技術含[[MIM]]金屬射出。
